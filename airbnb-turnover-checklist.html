Free guide for hosts

The Airbnb turnover checklist

Everything that should happen between one guest leaving and the next arriving — room by room, in the order that works. Use it yourself, or hand it to whoever cleans your property.

Go to the checklist

Room by room

Tick each item off as you go. Items marked The Cleanic are included in every turnover we do — the rest are usually handled by the host.

  1. 01Bedrooms
  2. 02Bathrooms
  3. 03Kitchen
  4. 04Living areas & floors
  5. 05Before the next guest
01

Bedrooms

  • Strip beds and remake with fresh linen The Cleanic
  • Check under beds and in drawers for left-behind items
  • Dust bedside tables, headboards and surfaces The Cleanic
  • Vacuum floors and rugs The Cleanic
  • Check pillows and duvets for marks; rotate as needed
02

Bathrooms

  • Clean and sanitise toilet, basin, shower and tiles The Cleanic
  • Wipe mirrors, taps and fixtures The Cleanic
  • Set out fresh towels The Cleanic
  • Top up toilet paper, soap and toiletries
  • Check drains and extractor fan
03

Kitchen

  • Wipe benches and splashbacks The Cleanic
  • Clean the sink and appliance fronts The Cleanic
  • Empty rubbish and recycling The Cleanic
  • Check the fridge for leftover food
  • Run and empty the dishwasher; put everything back where guests expect it
  • Top up coffee, tea and basics
04

Living areas & floors

  • Wipe tables, surfaces and furniture The Cleanic
  • Vacuum and mop floors The Cleanic
  • Straighten cushions, throws and chairs
  • Check remotes, chargers and the Wi-Fi card are in place
05

Before the next guest

  • Walk through as if you were the guest arriving
  • Note anything broken, damaged or running low
  • Check heating or cooling, lights and windows
  • Confirm keys, lockbox or access details for the next booking

Four habits of hosts with consistent turnovers

Leave enough time

Block a cleaning window between check-out and check-in in your calendar, so the turnover is never rushed.

Keep a spare set

Have at least two full sets of linen and towels per bed, so one set can be washed while the other is in use.

Same order, every time

Working through rooms in the same order is how nothing gets missed — the guest should never be able to tell which stay they are.

Photograph your standard

Take photos of each room set up exactly how you want it. It makes the standard easy to repeat and to share with whoever cleans.
