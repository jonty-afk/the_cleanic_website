How a turnover works

The four stages of an Airbnb turnover clean — from check-out to guest-ready.

Read the transcript
  1. How a turnover works.
  2. 01 Check-out — Your guest leaves. The clean is lined up for the gap between stays.
  3. 02 Reset — Beds stripped and remade with fresh linen. Towels replaced. Rubbish out.
  4. 03 Clean — Kitchen and bathrooms cleaned and sanitised, surfaces wiped, floors done.
  5. 03 Clean — Every room, the same way, every time.
  6. 04 Guest-ready — The property is presented and ready for the next arrival.

What every turnover covers

  4. Kitchen: Benches and splashbacks wiped down; Sink and appliances cleaned; Rubbish removed.
