Services · Auckland

Cleaning, properly done.

Airbnb and short-stay turnovers are our focus. We also take on deeper cleans, move-outs and specialist jobs across Auckland.

Our speciality

Airbnb & short-stay turnovers

Cleaning and resetting short-stay properties between guests — beds, bathrooms, kitchen and floors — with linen service and guest toiletries available.
