Spring cleaning, top to bottom

A thorough seasonal clean for the build-up that collects behind, under and on top of things.

Get a quote From $180

Overview

Over time, dust and grime settle in the places a regular clean doesn’t reach. A spring clean goes after them — behind furniture, under appliances, on top of shelves — and leaves the whole property feeling reset.

Many people book one or two a year, at the change of season or before a special occasion.

What a spring clean covers

  • Behind furniture and under appliances
  • High shelves and hard-to-reach areas
  • Oven and fridge cleaned inside
  • Interior windows
  • Skirting boards and ceiling fans dusted
  • Full bathroom deep clean — tiles, fixtures and surfaces

Suited to

  • Seasonal refreshes
  • Before hosting or a special occasion
  • Homes that are due a proper reset

Pricing

From $180. Starting prices. Final pricing depends on property size, condition, access and cleaning scope.

Questions

How much does a spring clean cost?

Spring cleaning starts from $180. The final price depends on the size and condition of the property, access and scope.

How often should I book one?

Twice a year works well for most homes — or whenever the place needs a proper reset.
